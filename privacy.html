We use the Pinterest API exclusively to automatically publish promotional pins for Art&Fun designs to the public boards of the Art&Fun Pinterest account. We only access the Art&Fun account data: public profile, boards, pins, images, titles, descriptions, shop links and public performance metrics. We do not collect, store or process any data of other Pinterest users.

Les métadonnées des épingles (titres, descriptions, liens d'images, calendrier de publication) sont stockées localement sur l'ordinateur du propriétaire du compte. Aucune donnée n'est transférée vers un serveur externe contrôlé par Art&Fun.

Pin metadata (titles, descriptions, image links, schedule) is stored locally on the account owner's computer. No data is transferred to an external server controlled by Art&Fun.

Data is not shared, sold or transmitted to third parties, except to Pinterest as necessary to publish pins.
